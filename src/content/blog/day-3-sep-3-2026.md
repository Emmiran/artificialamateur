Today I decided to get straight into a new project. Not something that would be long term, recurring or a real business, but something that I felt could be automated to a high level by AI.

I wanted to attempt to register and sell domain names, or bundles of domain names to OnlyFans models. To be clear, I don't have any real love or hate for that platform, but I'm certainly not above attempting to make a bit of money from it.

My plan was to register 'Only' + 'Name' domains, so for example OnlyDawn.com. I felt these could be of high value and demand for lead generation, user funneling and brand protection. I also needed to decide which from the plethora of other TLD options had value. Claude suggested that as well as the .com, .net, .co, .live, .fan, .xxx all had potential, but only if the .com was available.

My next task was to attempt to compile a huge list of usernames on the OF platform, to rank them in order of name popularity to have a priority list of trying to secure domains. This is where I hit a major hurdle. The mainstream AIs won't access any such information which hasn't been provided without consent, and they go to lengths to frown upon using similar data on third party sites which has been gathered this way. I tried a few variations of the request and ran into the same roadblocks.

So I changed my order. I asked Claude to generate a list of the 500 most common female names in the world. It did this. I then started searching a domain registrar for availability on the domain names I was targeting in .com, and I found very few were actually available. This, coupled with the issues I was having getting a consolidated list of usernames, plus second guessing myself around the ethics of the consent side of things, I decided to kill the project. I understand why they do it, both because the content is of an adult nature, and also they don't want to be grabbing things that are paid and reposting for free, but that wasn't really my use case. I reserve the right to revive the project if I have a brainwave of how to make it work. This also meant I dumped over an hour of recorded footage of me working through it, so the recording of my work isn't going terribly well. At least I remembered to turn the mic on!

I turned my attention to the blog. I added a projects killed item to the scrolling info bar. I halved the size of the footer section of the site as it is a different colour and over sized for what it currently contained. I changed the favicon ( the small image at the left hand side of a browser tab ) from the default image it was to the AA from the site logo. I just asked Claude to do it, and wasn't sure if it would, given that it required clipping an image etc, but it did it fine.

I then gave Claude the question 'Can you change the design of the main section of each website page to have a faint circuit board design, similar to that contained on the logo' I was pretty confident it wouldn't be able to do this, and I would have to mock something up in a image generator but it understood it straight away and produced a result that was pretty close to what i had in mind, just to give the page a bit more visual interest. Very impressed.

Overall my progress has been pretty embarrassing to date, but tomorrow I'll start on a much larger project. It is unlikely to yield any short term results though, but I'm interested to see what I can do, even for interest's sake.
